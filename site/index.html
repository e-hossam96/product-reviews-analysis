What it does

  • You upload a CSV with a text column: one product, up to 100 reviews.
  • The model suggests the product name and the aspects customers talk about, such as battery life or price. You check and edit them.
  • Every review gets a score from 1 to 5 for each aspect it mentions. You see the averages in a chart and can download the result as a CSV.
